How It Works

  1. 1

    Parse & anonymize

    Text is pulled from each file, then name, email, phone, address, and age are stripped before scoring begins.

  2. 2

    Score, transparently

    Skills, experience, and education are matched against your stated requirements with fixed, readable weights — no trained black box.

  3. 3

    A person decides

    Reviewers see the full breakdown and resume text, then record Advance, Hold, Reject, or Request Info — logged with their name.
